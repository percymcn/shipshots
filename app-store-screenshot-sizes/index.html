Format rules Apple enforces

Get any of these wrong and App Store Connect rejects the upload.

File type

PNG or JPEG. No HEIC, no PDF.

Color

RGB, sRGB or Display P3. Flattened — no alpha / transparency.

Count

Up to 10 screenshots per display size, in the order you set.

What changed in 2025

Apple dropped the requirement to upload a separate set for every device. Give it the 6.9" iPhone (1320×2868) and 13" iPad (2064×2752), and App Store Connect scales those to every smaller listing automatically. Dedicated 6.5" and 12.9" sets are now optional, and 5.5" is no longer accepted for new required slots.

FAQ

What sizes does the App Store require in 2026?

Just two base sizes: iPhone 6.9" at 1320×2868 px and iPad 13" at 2064×2752 px (portrait). Apple scales them down to fill smaller device listings, so a 5.5" or 12.9" set is no longer needed.

What is the iPhone 6.9-inch screenshot size?

1320×2868 pixels portrait, 2868×1320 landscape. It's the required iPhone base and covers iPhone 16 Pro Max, 15 Pro Max, and 14 Pro Max.

What is the iPad 13-inch screenshot size?

2064×2752 pixels portrait, 2752×2064 landscape — the required base for the 13" iPad Pro (M4).

Do I still need 6.5-inch and 12.9-inch screenshots?

No — still accepted, no longer required. Upload only the 6.9" iPhone and 13" iPad sets and App Store Connect fills the rest by scaling. Dedicated 6.5" (1242×2688) or 12.9" (2048×2732) uploads are optional.

What format do App Store screenshots need to be?

PNG or JPEG, RGB, flattened with no transparency (no alpha channel), sRGB or Display P3. Up to 10 per display size.
