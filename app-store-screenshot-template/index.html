A good App Store screenshot template is really three things: the two required artboard sizes, a caption-safe zone for your headline, and a device frame. Here is the exact spec — and why a generated template beats a static Figma file every time Apple moves the required sizes.

Anatomy of a template

Four layers, stacked. Get the artboard size right and the rest is composition.

1

Artboard at the required size

Lock the canvas to the base pixel size: iPhone 6.9" at 1320×2868 and iPad 13" at 2064×2752 (portrait). Everything else scales from these two. The full per-device table is on App Store screenshot sizes.

2

Device frame layer

Drop your captured app screen into a device bezel. Frames are optional, but a framed screen reads as a real product in the search carousel far better than a raw full-bleed capture.

3

Caption-safe zone

Reserve the top ~25% for a short benefit headline. Most impressions happen at thumbnail size in search, so the caption — not the pixels below it — carries the click.

4

Background layer

A flat color or subtle gradient behind the frame keeps the set cohesive across all screens. Export the whole thing flattened — no alpha — so App Store Connect accepts it.
